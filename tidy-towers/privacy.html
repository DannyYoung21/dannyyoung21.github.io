This policy applies to the game “Tidy Towers” for Android and for iPhone and iPad (package and bundle ID com.dannylabs.tidytowers).

2. In short

  • Tidy Towers has no user accounts and no server of its own. Your progress stays on your device (and, if applicable, in your own backup with Google or Apple, see section 7).
  • The game shows video ads only when you choose to watch one (for example, for an extra life). So that a video is ready, the game loads one in advance through Google AdMob.
  • The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure how it is played (for example, which levels many players fail) so we can improve it. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland only with your consent; everywhere you can turn it off.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see payment details.
  • You can change your choices in the game at any time: Settings → Privacy (ads) and Settings → Game stats (statistics).

3. Consent

On first launch, where the law of your country requires it (for example in the EU, the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent. The form comes from Google’s “User Messaging Platform”. You can accept, decline, or choose individual purposes. Your choice is stored on the device until you delete the app, and you can change or withdraw it at any time under Settings → Privacy.

Independently of that, you can turn game stats off and on again in every country under Settings → Game stats.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

After launch (and after every video shown), the game loads a video in advance through Google AdMob so that it is ready. It is shown only if you choose it. Even while loading, Google processes, among other things:

  • your device’s advertising ID (only for personalized ads; on Android only if you have not deleted it, on iPhone and iPad only if you allow it in Apple’s tracking prompt – see below),
  • a device identifier that applies only to this app or to apps from the same developer (on Android the app set ID, on iPhone and iPad the identifier for vendors), also without consent, for example for frequency capping and fraud prevention,
  • the IP address,
  • information about the device and the app (model, operating system, language),
  • your approximate location, estimated from the IP address,
  • information on whether and how the video was shown.
  • With consent, the videos may be tailored to your interests.
  • Without consent, Google shows “limited” ads: not personalized and with less data. A video cannot be delivered with no data processing at all, among other things for fraud prevention.

Google is an independent controller for the ads (Google’s controller-controller terms). Legal basis: for personalized ads, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); for limited ads, our legitimate interest in funding the free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). How long Google keeps ad data is governed by Google’s policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy

On iPhone and iPad, the game also uses Apple’s tracking prompt (“App Tracking Transparency”) to ask whether it may use the advertising ID (IDFA). iOS releases it only if you allow it there. Without your permission, Google does not receive it, and ads are not tailored based on your activity in other apps and on websites. You can change your choice at any time in the iOS settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

On Android, you can reset or delete the advertising ID in the settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Game stats (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Provider: Google Ireland Limited (see above).

The game sends game events to Google Analytics for Firebase:

  • in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland only if you consent;
  • in other countries as long as you do not turn game stats off (Settings → Game stats).

What is sent:

  • start and end of a level (level number, won, lost, or quit, wave reached, stars, duration, continues used),
  • videos offered and watched, continues offered and used,
  • opening the shop and purchases (which product; for purchases through Google Play and the App Store, Firebase may also record price and currency automatically),
  • whether the game tips were read,
  • running out of lives.

In addition, a random ID of the app installation, device type, operating system, language, approximate country, usage time, and events such as first launch, session start, and app update are collected automatically. We do not use the advertising ID for statistics. On iPhone and iPad, game stats do not collect the identifier for vendors (IDFV) either.

Here Google acts as a processor on our behalf (Google’s data processing terms). The legal basis is your consent in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), and otherwise our legitimate interest in a good game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), which you can object to at any time with the switch.

We use the data only to improve the game (difficulty, help, offers). It is not sold. Google keeps it for at most 14 months.

If game stats are off or your consent is missing, nothing is sent.

6. Purchases (Google Play and App Store)

Purchases in the game go through Google Play on Android and through Apple’s App Store on iPhone and iPad. Google or Apple processes your payment and account data under its own privacy policy. We only receive a confirmation of which product was bought and a purchase ID (legal basis: contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The game stores this ID on your device so that no purchase is credited twice.

7. Progress

Your progress (levels, stars, lives, purchased items) and your settings (for example language and sound) are stored on your device. If you use Android’s backup, Android also copies them to your own Google backup so they come back after you switch devices or reinstall; you control this in Android’s settings. On iPhone and iPad, they are part of your iCloud or computer backup if you use one; you control this in the iOS settings. We never receive them.

8. Transfers abroad

Google may also transfer data to countries outside Switzerland and the EEA, in particular to the USA. For this, Google relies on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses and on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework.

9. Children

Tidy Towers is intended for adults. It is not directed at children.

10. Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access,
  • rectification,
  • erasure,
  • restriction of processing,
  • objection,
  • data portability,
  • withdrawal of consent (under Settings → Privacy or Settings → Game stats).

Because the game has no accounts, we usually cannot find you in the data held by Google ourselves. Write to us at dannylabs.studio@gmail.com and we will help as best we can. You can also contact a supervisory authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the authority of your country of residence.
